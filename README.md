# Rennova

**Create. Edit. Bring Your Vision to Life.**

Rennova is a video editing application designed to make creating and editing videos simple, efficient, and accessible.

Whether you're editing YouTube videos, personal projects, or professional content, Rennova provides a streamlined workspace to bring your ideas to life.

## About

Rennova focuses on video editing rather than advanced motion graphics compositing. It aims to provide an intuitive editing experience with a clean interface and practical tools.

## Features

Rennova aims to offer:

- **Timeline Editing** — Arrange, trim, and organize video clips.
- **Audio Editing** — Add and manage audio tracks.
- **Transitions** — Create smooth transitions between clips.
- **Text and Titles** — Add captions and titles to videos.
- **Video Preview** — Preview your edits while working.
- **Exporting** — Render and export completed videos.
- **Simple Interface** — Work in a clean, distraction-free environment.

*Features listed above are planned capabilities and may not all be available yet.*
